Pediatric Physiotherapy

Relaxation Therapy

Breathing, progressive muscle relaxation, and guided regulation routines for stress/pain support.

Testing and Follow-up

Structured functional assessment workflows to establish baselines and track progression over time.

Testing Framework (Named, Standardized)

Testing tools support clinical reasoning and communication. They do not replace medical diagnosis or urgent referral decisions.
Berg Balance Scale (BBS)

Adult balance screening and progress tracking in functional tasks.

Pediatric Balance Scale (PBS)

School-age child functional balance observation with 14-item scoring.

Timed Up and Go (TUG)

Simple mobility transition timing for adults and children in context.

Visual Analogue Scale (VAS)

Pain intensity anchor for adults and pediatric-adapted communication flows.
